## Getting started

1. **Create your account** on the sign-up page (name, email, password of at least 10 characters).
2. **Create your association**: give it a name, choose your position on the board, and keep the
   suggested starter kit (founding checklist and a few poles). Everything can be changed later.
3. **Invite the others**: go to **Members → Invite people**, create a join code and share its link.
   People who open the link create their account and land directly in your association.

If someone gave you a code, enter it when you sign up, or later from **Create or join an association**
(association menu at the top of the sidebar).

## Roles

Each person has a role _in each association_:

| Role              | Can do                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Member**        | See everything, create tasks and requests, take requests for their poles, comment, vote, log time, RSVP, add documents. Edit tasks they created or are assigned to, and projects they lead. |
| **Manager**       | Everything a member does, plus manage poles, projects, events, the founding checklist, finance, announcements and join codes (for the Member role).                                         |
| **Administrator** | Everything, plus members, roles, the association settings and backups.                                                                                                                      |

Pole **leads** can edit their pole, its projects, and give requests to someone in their pole.
The **board position** (president, treasurer, secretary…) is separate from the role: it appears on the
organization chart, and secretaries and presidents can write meeting minutes.

## Founding checklist

**Founding** lists the steps to create an association (written for a French "association loi 1901",
easy to adapt): purpose and name, statutes, constitutive general assembly, board election, declaration,
publication, bank account, insurance, membership fee, poles, communication…

- Tick a step to mark it done; open it to see details, notes and a useful link.
- Assign each step to someone and give it a date, so everyone knows who does what.
- Mark a step **Skipped** if it does not apply to you, or add your own steps.
- The dashboard shows your progress until everything is done.

## Structure

**Structure** shows how the association is organized:

- the **organization chart**: the association, its board (vacant seats included) and its poles with their leads;
- **Who does what**: recurring duties (keep the accounts, answer emails, renew the insurance…) with an owner,
  a pole and a frequency, so nothing is forgotten when people change;
- the **membership matrix**: who is in which pole (★ = lead) and how many open tasks each person has.

## Poles

A pole is a working group (communication, events, partnerships, treasury…). Each pole page shows its team,
projects, open tasks, requests, duties and upcoming events. Managers and leads add members and choose leads.

## Requests

Requests are how a pole asks another one for help ("can someone design a poster?").

1. Click **New request** (or **Ask this pole** on a pole page), choose the pole, describe what you need,
   and optionally a due date, a project and the pole you are asking for.
2. **Every member of that pole is notified** (bell icon, and email if configured).
3. One of them clicks **Take it**: a task is created and assigned to them. You are notified.
   Pole leads and managers can also **Give to…** a specific member.
4. When the task is marked done, the request is closed and **you are notified**.

The person who took a request can **release** it (the task is deleted and the request goes back to the pole).
The requester can **cancel** a request until someone takes it. The **For my poles** tab shows what is
waiting for you; the dashboard highlights it too.

## Projects

Each project has a lead, a team, a pole, dates, a budget and a color.

- **Progress** is computed from tasks and milestones (a milestone weighs two tasks), or set manually.
- **Health** compares time spent with progress: _off track_ when the due date has passed, _at risk_ when
  progress is more than 25 points behind schedule.
- **Updates** let the team post a short report with a health status; the latest one appears on the overview.
- **Budget** shows expenses linked to the project against its budget.
- The **Timeline** view on the projects page shows all projects on a calendar with today's line.

## Tasks

The **Tasks** page has a board (drag cards between _To do_, _In progress_, _In review_ and _Done_) and a list.
Filter by person, project, pole or priority. Each task has comments, a due date (overdue tasks show in red)
and a **Log time** button to record the volunteer time spent on it.

## Events and meetings

Create general assemblies, board meetings, pole meetings, workshops or public events. Members answer
_Going / Maybe / Not going_. Meetings have an **agenda** and **minutes**: keep the minutes of general
assemblies and board meetings, they are the official record of your decisions. Tick **Notify members**
when creating an event to notify everyone (or the pole's members).

To keep a paper copy (or a PDF), print the page of the meeting: only the agenda, the minutes and the
attendance are printed, without the menu or buttons. The finance page and the task board print the same way.

To see events in your own calendar, go to **Settings → Notifications → Calendar subscription** and add the
private link to Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook ("subscribe from URL").

## Votes

Anyone can open a vote: a question, two or more options (the **Yes / No / Abstain** button fills them in),
an optional closing date, single or multiple choice, anonymous or not, and optionally only for a pole.
Members are notified. You can change your vote until it closes. The creator and managers can close or delete it.

## Volunteering

Members log the time they give (`1h30`, `45min`, `2.5`…), optionally linked to a pole, project, task or event.
The page shows your hours and the association's, totals by member and by pole, and an **estimated value**
(set the value of one hour in **Settings → Association**). Export a CSV for grant applications or your
annual report.

## Finance

Record income and expenses with a category, payment method, reference, project and pole. The page shows
the year's totals, a monthly chart, expenses by category and project budgets. **Membership fees** lists who
paid for the year; recording a payment also adds the matching income. Everything can be exported as CSV.

## Documents

Upload files (statutes, minutes, receipts, guides…) or save links, with a category, pole or project.
Files are only accessible to members of the association. Photos are reduced to 1600 pixels when they are
uploaded (sharp on screen, light to store and download), and their location data is removed.

## Members and invitations

The **Members** page lists everyone with their role, board position, poles, membership fee status and
account status. Administrators can:

- **Add a member** directly (with or without an invitation link). If the person already has an Agora
  account, they are simply added to the association.
- Create **join codes** in **Invite people**: choose the number of uses, an expiry date, the role given,
  and optionally a pole to join automatically. Share the link (`…/join?code=XXXXX-XXXXX`); revoke a code at
  any time.
- Deactivate or remove members, and export the list as CSV.

## Notifications

The bell in the top bar shows what concerns you: requests to your poles, requests you made being taken or
done, tasks assigned to you, comments on tasks you created, are assigned to or commented on, new events,
votes and announcements, new members.
Click a notification to open it. With email configured on the server, you receive **one summary a day at
most**, listing only what you have not already seen in Agora; nothing is sent when you have seen everything.
In **Settings → Notifications** you can choose each notification right away instead, or no emails.
Read notifications are removed after 90 days.

## Your account

**Settings → Account & security** lets you change your password (other devices are signed out), see the
devices signed in to your account and sign them out, leave an association, or delete your account.
If you forget your password, use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page (when email is configured), or ask
an administrator of your association for a reset link.

## Several associations

You can belong to several associations with the same account. Switch between them with the association menu
at the top of the sidebar. Each association only sees its own data.
