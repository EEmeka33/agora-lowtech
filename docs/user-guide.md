# User guide

How to use Agora day to day. Everything exists in English and French (links at the bottom of every page).
Agora works in any browser, on a computer or a phone, even an old one, and needs no app.

## A few habits of the interface

- Forms that create something are folded under a green title (for example **▶ New task**): click it to open.
- Deleting always asks for confirmation: click **Delete…**, then **Yes, delete**.
- After each action the page reloads and a message at the top confirms it.
- The bottom of every page shows its weight and how long the server took to build it.
- Pages are made to be printed: menus and buttons disappear on paper.

## Getting started

1. **Create your account** (name, email, a password of at least 10 characters: a short sentence is ideal).
2. **Create your association**: its name, your position on the board, and the starter kit (founding checklist
   and a few poles). Everything can be changed later.
3. **Invite the others**: *Members → Invite people* creates a join code with a link to share. People who open
   it create their account and arrive directly in your association.

Got a code? Enter it when you sign up, or later in *Create or join an association* (bottom of the menu).

## Roles

| Role | Can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Member** | See everything, create tasks and requests, take requests for their poles, comment, vote, log time, answer events, add documents; edit what they created or are assigned to. |
| **Manager** | Also manage poles, projects, events, the founding checklist, finance, announcements and join codes (for members). |
| **Administrator** | Everything, including members, roles, the association's settings and backups. |

Pole **leads** can edit their pole, its projects, and give its requests to someone. The **board position**
(president, treasurer, secretary…) is separate from the role; secretaries and presidents can write minutes.

## Founding checklist

*Founding* lists the steps to create an association (written for a French "association loi 1901", easy to
adapt): purpose and name, statutes, constitutive assembly, declaration, bank account, insurance… Open a step
(*Details*) to read it, mark it done, or edit it (person in charge, date, notes, useful link). Mark a step
**Skipped** if it doesn't apply to you. The dashboard shows the progress until everything is done.

## Structure and poles

*Structure* shows the organization chart (board, with vacant seats, and poles with their leads), **who does
what** (recurring duties with an owner and a frequency, so nothing is forgotten when people change) and the
**membership matrix** (★ lead, ● member).

A **pole** is a working group (workshops, repair café, communication…). Its page shows its team, requests,
projects, open tasks, duties and upcoming events.

## Requests

How a pole asks another one for help ("can someone design a poster?"):

1. *Requests → Ask a pole for help* (or *Ask …* on a pole's page): choose the pole, describe the need.
2. **Every member of that pole is notified.**
3. One of them clicks **Take it**: a task is created for them and you are notified. Pole leads and managers can
   also **give** the request to a member.
4. When the task is marked done, the request is closed and **you are notified**.

The person who took a request can **release** it (the task is deleted, the request goes back to the pole). The
requester can **cancel** it until someone takes it.

## Projects

A project has a lead, a team, a pole, dates, a budget, milestones and progress updates, in tabs.

- **Progress** is computed from tasks and milestones (a milestone weighs two tasks), or set by hand.
- **Health**: *off track* when the due date has passed, *at risk* when progress is more than 25 points behind
  the time elapsed.
- The **timeline** (*Projects → Timeline*) shows every project on a 12-month table.

## Tasks

*Tasks* shows a **board** with one column per status; the ◀ ▶ buttons move a card to the previous or next
column. Switch to the **list** with *View*. Filter by project, pole, person or priority. Done tasks stay on
the board for 30 days. On a task: comments, status buttons, and *Log time*.

## Events and meetings

General assemblies, board and pole meetings, workshops, public events. Members answer *Going / Maybe / Not
going*. Meetings have an **agenda** and **minutes**, the official record of your decisions: write them in
Agora, print the page, sign it. *Notify members* sends a notification when the event is created.

**In your own calendar**: *Settings → Notifications → Calendar subscription* gives a private link to add to any
calendar app ("subscribe from URL").

## Votes

A question, options (or *Yes / No / Abstain*), an optional closing date, single or multiple choice, anonymous or
not, everyone or one pole. Results appear once you have voted; you can change your vote until it closes.

## Volunteering

Log the time you give (`1h30`, `45min`, `2.5`…), optionally for a pole or project (or from a task or an
event). The page shows your hours, everyone's hours by member and pole, and an **estimated value** (set the
value of one hour in *Settings → Association*). Managers can export a CSV for grant applications.

## Finance

Income and expenses with category, payment method, reference, project and pole; totals of the year, a
monthly chart, expenses and income by category, project budgets. **Membership fees**: who paid this year;
recording a payment also adds the income. Every member can see the accounts; managers change them.

## Documents

Files (statutes, minutes, receipts) or links, by category. Big photos are reduced automatically. Files can
only be opened by members of the association. Keep only what is useful: everything stored must be backed up
and protected.

## Members and invitations

*Members* lists everyone with role, poles and fee status. Administrators can:

- **add a member** directly, with an **activation link** to send them (valid 7 days);
- create **join codes** (*Invite people*): number of uses, expiry, role, a pole to join automatically;
- from a member's page: create a password reset link, deactivate, remove.

## Notifications and email

*Notifications* (at the top) lists what concerns you: requests to your poles, your requests taken or done,
tasks assigned to you, comments, new events, votes, announcements, new members. The count is updated when you
open a page.

By email (if the server is configured for it), choose in *Settings → Notifications*: **one summary a day**
(default), one email per notification, or none.

## Your account

*Settings*: your profile and language; *Account & security*: password, signed-in devices (sign them out),
leave an association, delete your account. Forgot your password? Use the link on the sign-in page, or ask an
administrator for a reset link.
